Product overview

One system for deciding what to study, practise and explain next.

ViewCoach helps users move from collecting interview material to following a structured preparation process. It combines a private question library, spaced review, role-based roadmaps, mock interviews, interview goals and reusable evidence from real projects and experiences.

Its adaptive study planner turns due reviews, roadmap progress, recently difficult questions, deadlines and available study time into a clear daily plan. Recommendations remain deterministic, visible and explainable rather than being hidden behind an AI model.

Core product question

Given the user's goals, deadlines, progress, weak areas and evidence, what should they practise next and why?

Architecture

Decisions made for explainability, ownership and controlled growth.

Modular Django monolith

Questions, reviews, roadmaps, planning, mock interviews, goals and evidence have clear domain boundaries without premature distributed-system complexity.

PostgreSQL from the beginning

The application uses one relational data model across local, test and production environments, with no application SQLite fallback.

Concrete questions with typed detail

Shared question behaviour remains central while each interview format stores and presents the information relevant to that format.

Persisted daily planning

Each daily plan is stored as a stable snapshot, allowing users to complete recommendations, track study sessions and deliberately regenerate their plan.

Reusable personal evidence

Projects, decisions and behavioural stories are stored once and linked to different questions, roadmap topics and interview goals without duplicating the underlying facts.

Deterministic logic before AI

Review scheduling, prioritisation and readiness remain visible, testable and explainable independently of future AI features.
