Product overview

One system for deciding what to learn, practise and explain next.

ViewCoach turns disconnected interview material into a structured preparation process. Users can follow curated or imported learning paths, build a private question library, review weak areas, run timed mocks, prepare evidence from real experience and receive a focused daily plan.

The core planning logic remains visible and testable. Candidate activities are scored from due reviews, roadmap progress, weak areas, goals and available time, then selected under explicit constraints. AI is used only where it adds clear value: generating editable drafts from the user's own notes and answering Help Centre questions from trusted documentation with citations.

Core product question

Given the user's goals, deadlines, progress, weak areas, evidence and available time, what should they practise next and why?

Architecture

Decisions made for explainability, ownership and controlled growth.

Modular Django monolith

Questions, reviews, roadmaps, planning, mock interviews, goals, evidence and knowledge retrieval have clear domain boundaries without premature distributed-system complexity.

PostgreSQL as the shared data foundation

Relational product data and vector embeddings live in one production database, with pgvector supporting cosine-similarity retrieval for the Help Assistant.

Source-separated roadmaps

Curated ViewCoach curricula and user-owned YouTube roadmaps reuse the same progress models, while retaining separate routes, metadata, access rules and interfaces.

Constraint-based planning with a safe fallback

CP-SAT optimises the activity mix under time, duplication and workload constraints. A deterministic heuristic takes over if the optimiser is unavailable or cannot return a solution.

AI outputs remain bounded

Generated question cards must be grounded in user notes, pass structural validation and remain drafts. They do not silently become trusted study material.

Retrieval fails closed

The Help Assistant answers only from published trusted documents, validates cited source numbers and refuses when retrieval or generated context cannot support a confident answer.

Ownership is enforced at the data boundary

Questions, imported roadmaps, notes, evidence, review state and generation batches are scoped to the authenticated user, with authorization behaviour covered by tests.

Persisted plans and histories

Daily plans, reviews, mock sessions, query logs and generation batches create inspectable records instead of hiding important behaviour inside temporary requests.
